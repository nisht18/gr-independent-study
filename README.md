# gr-independent-study

Notes and study materials for an independent directed study into the physical and mathematical foundations of General Relativity.

## 📚 Core Curriculum & Textbooks

I follow along with a grad-level syllabus covering the following subtopics:
* **Differential Geometry:** Manifolds, tensors, covariant derivatives, and curvature.
* **Spacetime Physics:** The equivalence principle, Einstein field equations, and the stress-energy tensor.
* **Applications:** The Schwarzschild metric, black holes, gravitational waves, and cosmology.

1. **Primary Text:** *Spacetime and Geometry: An Introduction to General Relativity* by Sean M. Carroll.
2. **Supplementary Text:** *A First Course in General Relativity* by Bernard Schutz.
3. **Supplementary Lectures:** *Lectures on General Relativity* by Frederic P. Schuller (YouTube Series).

## 🎯 Core Objectives

* **Geometric Machinery:** Build intuition for differential geometry, mapping abstract tensor calculus directly to physical spacetime curvature.
* **Formalize Einstein's Field Equations:** Reconstruct complex textbook derivations step-by-step to understand how mass-energy dictates spacetime geometry.
* **Bridge Theory & Applications:** Apply the Schwarzschild and cosmological metrics to solve concrete problems in black hole physics and gravitational radiation.
